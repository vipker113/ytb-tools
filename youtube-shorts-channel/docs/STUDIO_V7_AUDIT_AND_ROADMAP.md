# Proof of Wonder — Studio v7.0 Audit & Technical Improvement Roadmap

## Part 1, Section 1 — Complete Technical & Creative Audit of Existing Workflow (`Studio v6.0`)

### Inspected Repositories & Modules
- **Channel Production Engine:** `youtube-shorts-channel/pipeline/pow_studio/` (`voice_studio.py`, `audio_engine.py`, `sfx_library.py`, `shot_planner.py`, `visual_intelligence.py`, `story_engine.py`, `script_linter.py`, `style_profile.py`, `schema.py`, `compositor.py`, `qc_suite.py`, `manifest_v6.py`, `deliverables.py`, `cli.py`)
- **Configuration & Presets:** `youtube-shorts-channel/config/proof_of_wonder_style.yaml`, `config/profiles/*.yaml`, `config/voice_profiles/*.yaml`
- **Legacy Reference Check:** `MoneyPrinterTurbo` is excluded in `.gitignore` (`# Third-party legacy repo (Studio v6 is self-contained inside youtube-shorts-channel)`) and absent on disk; `pow_studio` is the sole active production engine.

---

### Root-Cause Findings Across Voice, Sound Design, Mixing & Story Editing

#### 1. ElevenLabs Integration & Model Configuration (`voice_studio.py`)
- **Finding:** Despite ElevenLabs being the preferred voice platform, `voice_studio.py` only called `edge_tts.Communicate`. There was **no ElevenLabs API client**, no model-capability matrix (`eleven_multilingual_v2` vs `eleven_v3` vs `eleven_turbo_v2_5` vs `eleven_flash_v2_5`), no `previous_text` / `next_text` continuity request stitching, no pronunciation dictionary support, and no guard preventing unsupported parameters (such as sending `style` or `use_speaker_boost` to `eleven_flash_v2_5`, or sending `[audio_tags]` as literal spoken text to `eleven_multilingual_v2`).

#### 2. Voice Selection, Annotations & Emotional Contrast (`voice_studio.py` L137–410, L1381–1416)
- **Finding A (Missing Profiles & Parameters):** Missing `calm_exploration` and `urgent_discovery` presets from Section 3; segment directives lacked `emotional_tone`, `pitch_direction`, `sentence_rhythm`, `pause_before_ms`, `pronunciation_overrides`, `emotional_intensity`, and `vocal_consistency`.
- **Finding B (Monotony vs. Over-Drama):** `export_annotated_voice_script()` stamped `MEASURED_EVIDENCE` (`energy=3`) onto every middle segment regardless of narrative meaning. Conversely, if a creator marked every line `[DELIVERY: CINEMATIC REVEAL]`, no contrast analyzer rebalanced the script—causing over-acted, fatiguing delivery with zero contrast.

#### 3. Sentence Segmentation, Concatenation & Timing Authority (`voice_studio.py` L1430–1536)
- **Finding A (Artificial Metallic Slapback):** `_apply_vocal_studio_master_chain()` (L1517) applied `aecho=0.88:0.88:16:0.042` (`16 ms` delay) to narration, introducing comb-filtering and an artificial "small bathroom / robotic TTS" coloration.
- **Finding B (Hard Array Cuts & No Continuity Audit):** `_trim_silence()` cut raw waveform arrays at `-47 dBFS` and concatenated zero-arrays directly without cosine micro-fades at boundaries, risking clipped plosives/fricatives, and had no post-concatenation continuity reviewer measuring inter-segment loudness/spectral jumps or preferring single-pass generation when prosody is uniform.
- **Finding C (No Dependent Cache Invalidation):** Regenerating narration overwrote `word_timings.json` without invalidating downstream `semantic_timeline_manifest.json`, `shot_plan_audit.json`, `subtitles.srt`, `voice_approval_lock.json`, or cached `shots/*.mp4`.

#### 4. Background Music Selection & Template Drone (`audio_engine.py` L145–201)
- **Finding:** `_build_layer_2_music_underscore()` synthesized the exact same 4-sine-wave drone (`f_root`, `1.5*f_root`, `1.5874*f_root`, `2.0*f_root` low-passed at `390 Hz`) with hardcoded 3-act ramps (`act2_rise` at `u=0.25`, `act3_climax` at `u=0.55`) across 100% of every video's duration. Every episode sounded musically identical, could not drop music during dense scientific explanations or pre-reveal silences, and never chose a 0-music, 1-section, or multi-section progression based on the story.

#### 5. Sound Effects, Environmental Ambience & Licensing Registry (`audio_engine.py` L204–262, L539–579; `sfx_library.py`)
- **Finding A (`gate_sfx` Bypass Bug):** In `audio_engine.py` L562, `gate_mult = 1.0 if cue.anchor_type in ("shot_start", "seg_start", "time_s") else ...` evaluated to `1.0` for **every valid `SfxCueSpec`**, bypassing `scene_audio_layers` exclusions!
- **Finding B (No Visual/Story Context Check):** SFX cues were placed at shot starts without verifying whether the visible image showed the implied physical action, whether a transition needed sound at all, or whether silence was stronger.
- **Finding C (Cut-Fragmented Ambience & No Audio Registry):** Ambience gating stepped per shot instead of grouping connected scenes into continuous environmental beds, and there was no dedicated `audio_asset_registry.json` tracking verified CC0/Public Domain audio sources, creators, licenses, technical specs, and `authentic_field_recording` vs `first_party_acoustic_synthesis` provenance.

#### 6. Audio Mixing, Ducking & Destructive Upward Normalization (`audio_engine.py` L495–595)
- **Finding:** `_calibrate_bed_stem_lufs()` (L574–578) summed `music + ambience + nat + sfx` and forced the combined stem up or down by up to `+16.0 dB` to hit a hardcoded `target_bed_lufs` (`-26.5 LUFS`). In a restrained video with no music and quiet ambience, this **boosted background hiss/SFX by `+16 dB`**, actively punishing restraint! Furthermore, ducking used one uniform depth for all speech rather than configurable phrase-importance ducking for key reveals.

#### 7. Fixed-Duration Assumptions, Scene Selection, Redundancy & QA (`schema.py`, `shot_planner.py`, `script_linter.py`, `compositor.py`, `qc_suite.py`)
- **Finding A (Zero Redundant Scene Removal):** `resolve_timed_shots()` blindly stretched/compressed every shot in `manifest.shots` to match `total_frames_30fps`. There was no `Keep` / `Replace` / `Merge` / `Remove` editor detecting duplicate visual beats, filler establishing shots, repeated sub-clips of the same asset, or rapid cuts shorter than viewer comprehension time.
- **Finding B (Shorts-Only Duration & Loop Assumptions):** `EpisodeManifest` defaulted to `target_duration_min_s=48.0, target_duration_max_s=90.0` (failing a 35s Short or 150s documentary in Gate 1!); `script_linter.py` enforced a `<=0.55s` seamless-loop tail pause even on 2.5-minute documentaries; `qc_suite.py` L711 failed episodes when `voice_to_ambience_margin_LU > 16.5 LU`, wrongly penalizing minimal/no-music episodes!
- **Finding C (Cross-Platform Draft Encoder Bug):** `compositor.py` L137 hardcoded macOS-only `h264_videotoolbox` in `draft` mode, failing on Windows/Linux.
- **Finding D (Technical-Only Pass Risk):** `qc_suite.py` checked waveform/container metrics without a severity-graded Creative QA audit for voice contrast, audio-visual semantic match, unnecessary SFX, or unpruned scene redundancy.
