## YouTube Description (Ready to Copy-Paste — Includes Required Media Credits)

Thirty-three kilometers off São Paulo, Brazil, Ilha da Queimada Grande (Snake Island) holds roughly 2,000 to 4,000 golden lancehead pit vipers (Bothrops insularis)—up to 90 vipers per hectare in prime forest habitat according to Instituto Butantan surveys. While there is no evidence for viral rumors claiming five snakes per square meter, isolated vipers adapted to ambush migratory birds in the canopy with hemotoxic venom up to five times more potent against avian prey.
