- **NOAA National Ocean Service — What is the Bermuda Triangle? & Puerto Rico Trench Bathymetry (Milwaukee Deep ~8,376 m).** — https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/bermudatri.html  
  *Supports:* No evidence that mysterious disappearances occur with greater frequency in the Bermuda Triangle than in any other large, well-traveled area of the ocean; Gulf Stream rapid weather/wave changes; deep trenches including the Puerto Rico Trench.
- **U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) — Gas Hydrates and the Bermuda Triangle Myth.** — https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-bermuda-triangle  
  *Supports:* USGS gas-hydrate research shows no large methane-hydrate releases have occurred in the Bermuda Triangle / Blake Ridge region for roughly the past 15,000 years.
- **Frazer, W. D., Park, J., & Olugboji, T. — Seismic receiver-function imaging of a ~20-km-thick low-density underplated rock layer beneath Bermuda supporting the ~500-m Bermuda Swell.** — https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/19448007  
  *Supports:* Identification of an unusual ~20-kilometer-thick underplated rock layer beneath Bermuda's oceanic crust whose buoyancy holds the surrounding Bermuda seafloor swell ~500 meters higher than expected without an active hot-spot plume.
- **NOAA Ocean Exploration — Identification of the Wreck of SS Cotopaxi off St. Augustine, Florida (2020).** — https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/  
  *Supports:* SS Cotopaxi vanished on a voyage from Charleston to Havana in December 1925 during a severe Atlantic storm and was positively identified underwater ~35 nautical miles off St. Augustine, Florida.
