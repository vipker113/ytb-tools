## Final Verbatim Spoken Narration (Word-for-Word Match to `narration.mp3`)

Mars has barely any air. So how did NASA fly a helicopter there?

Mars's atmosphere is only about one percent as dense as Earth's. That makes generating lift incredibly difficult.

Meet Ingenuity, a tiny aircraft weighing just 1.8 kilograms.

Ingenuity used two counter-rotating rotors, spinning at roughly 2,500 revolutions per minute, to push enough air downward.

NASA planned just five test flights.

Ingenuity ended up flying 72 times.

A small experiment designed to prove one thing became the first aircraft to achieve powered, controlled flight on another planet.
