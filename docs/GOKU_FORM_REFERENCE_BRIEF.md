# GOKU FORM REFERENCE — KI EVOLUTION GENERATION BRIEF

> **Purpose:** Visual reference and generation brief for an AI image/video agent.  
> **Scope:** Main continuity forms only. **Great Ape / Oozaru is intentionally excluded.**  
> **Important:** The reference images below are copyrighted Dragon Ball artwork/promotional images. Use them to study visual traits and continuity. Do not assume they are licensed for commercial reuse. For a monetized channel, obtain appropriate rights or use this document only as a visual study and create original characters.

## 0. Global art-direction lock

### Target visual language
Use the **classic Dragon Ball Z / Dragon Ball Super anime character-design language**, not a generic modern anime look.

- Clean, confident anime ink outlines; controlled line-weight variation.
- Strong, simple silhouette; spiky hair built from distinct large hair clumps, not hundreds of thin strands.
- Toriyama-style compact facial construction: simple nose, expressive angular eyes, clear brow shapes, strong jaw on adult Goku.
- Athletic martial-artist anatomy. Keep proportions consistent between adult forms.
- Flat cel-shading with a small number of decisive shadow shapes; avoid painterly skin, photorealism, 3D render, glossy plastic, excessive gradients.
- Goku's recognizable orange gi, dark-blue undershirt, blue wristbands, blue belt/sash and blue boots unless a specific reference scene requires a damaged outfit.
- Neutral studio background for character sheets. No typography, logos, watermarks, UI, power-level labels or other characters.
- Keep camera, body scale, pose, lighting and framing consistent across all forms so the images can be compared as one evolution lineup.

### Identity consistency rules
- Same adult Goku face, adult body proportions, outfit construction and camera angle from Adult Base through Mastered Ultra Instinct.
- Kid Goku is a separate age/body proportion reference: shorter child body, rounder face, larger head-to-body ratio. Do not make him look like a miniature adult.
- Change only the form-specific features listed in each stage. Do not invent extra hair colors, extra transformations, armor, tattoos, horns or costume redesigns.
- Hair color and aura color must remain distinct: aura effects must not recolor the skin or outfit unless the source form explicitly does so.
- Do not combine Super Saiyan 4, Dragon Ball GT, Dragon Ball Heroes, fan-made forms or Dragon Ball DAIMA's separate SS4 design into this sequence.

### Recommended generation setup
- Generate one image per form, not a collage.
- Aspect ratio: **3:4 portrait** for character reference sheets.
- Framing: full body, front-facing or slight 3/4 view, feet visible, centered.
- Pose: relaxed iconic fighting stance, fists lightly clenched; no attack pose that hides the silhouette.
- Output: high-resolution PNG, clean background.
- For consistency, generate Adult Base first and use it as the identity/pose reference for every adult form.

# 1. Core evolution sequence

1. **Stage 01 — Kid Goku (Original Dragon Ball, base)**
2. **Stage 02 — Adult Goku (Base Form)** *(Master identity reference)*
3. **Stage 03 — Super Saiyan** *(Golden upright spiky hair, turquoise eyes, eyebrows present, no lightning)*
4. **Stage 04 — Super Saiyan 2** *(Sharper golden spiky hair, eyebrows present, fierce expression, blue-white electrical arcs)*
5. **Stage 05 — Super Saiyan 3** *(Waist-length flowing golden hair, NO eyebrows, pronounced brow ridge)*
6. **Stage 06 — Super Saiyan God** *(Crimson-red hair, red eyes, slimmer athletic build, calm divine expression, red/pink flame aura)*
7. **Stage 07 — Super Saiyan Blue (SSGSS)** *(Vivid cyan-blue spiky hair, blue eyes, blue-white aura)*
   - *Optional special state — Super Saiyan Blue Kaioken (Cyan-blue hair + red Kaioken energy outer layer over blue-white aura)*
8. **Stage 08 — Ultra Instinct Sign / Omen** *(Black/dark hair, silver-grey eyes, calm focused expression, blue-white/silver aura)*
9. **Stage 09 — Mastered / Completed Ultra Instinct** *(Silver-white hair, silver eyes, calm effortless expression, silver-white aura)*
